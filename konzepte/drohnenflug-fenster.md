## Prompt (Entwurf, 10 s)
@Image1 is the first frame and the only reference, the video starts exactly on this image. One continuous drone shot, no cuts.
Layout: the freshly painted Berlin corner Altbau fills the frame, ochre walls, white window surrounds, metal scaffold with red-edged planks in front, evening daylight.
0–3 s: the drone glides smoothly straight toward the corner bay, the building grows, scaffold poles slide past the edges of the frame.
3–5 s: it passes through a gap between two scaffold poles at third-floor height and slows down in front of one tall white-framed double casement window, centered.
5–6.5 s: the two window wings swing open inward, the room behind is dark.
6.5–8 s: the drone flies slowly through the open window, the white window frame slides past both sides of the lens.
8–10 s: inside an empty high Altbau room with dark charcoal walls and a wooden floor; the drone glides toward a large white ornate molded picture frame on the far wall until the frame fills most of the image, seen straight on, dark empty inside.
Camera: one smooth forward movement, steady, gimbal-stabilized, no shake, no fast turns.
Style: photorealistic, natural light, sharp, cinematic.
Avoid: cuts, jump cuts, morphing building, extra windows appearing, people, text, logos, camera shake, motion blur smear.
