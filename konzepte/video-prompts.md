# FARBKANTE – Video-Prompts (One-Take, nur First Frame als Referenz)

Regeln: ein Clip, nur das 4K-First-Frame als einzige Bildreferenz, keine Naht, Sekunde für Sekunde.
Drafts zuerst kostenlos (Wan 3.0 Unlimited 480p / MiniMax H3 Unlimited 768p), Endrender 2K MiniMax H3 nur nach OK.
Recherche 19.11.2026 (Referenzen: Fassadenrenovierung beim Profi QVvpjVtIGbM, Fassaden-Zeitraffer ZX8z2MlIASQ, Fassade fertig fivghlRYgjU):
Ablauf Profi = Gerüst stellen → Risse/Putz → Tiefgrund → zweimal streichen → Netz/Gerüst runter. Zeitraffer = Kamera fest auf Stativ, nur Licht und Arbeit ändern sich. Rolle „muss schmatzen“ = satt Farbe drauf, glänzend nass.
Die Rolle über die Linse (Ende Kapitel Raum) kommt aus dem Code (m3-teal-rolle), nicht aus dem Video.

## Film 1 – Fassade (First Frame f1-geruest-netz, 10 s, Wan 3.0 Unlimited)

```
Locked-off static camera on a tripod, a true time-lapse, the camera never moves, never zooms, never cuts. Layout from left to right: calm dark neighbouring building and trees in the left third, the large five-storey old Berlin corner apartment building wrapped in steel scaffolding with white translucent safety netting in the center and right, quiet street in the foreground.
Second 0-2: blue hour dusk, warm work lights glow on the scaffold levels, the netting moves gently in the wind, a few windows glow warm yellow.
Second 2-4: night passes in time-lapse, the sky turns from deep blue to dark navy and then to a pale dawn blue, the work lights switch off one level after another from bottom to top.
Second 4-7: early morning light, the white safety netting is taken down level by level from the top floor to the ground floor, each net panel is lowered smoothly behind the scaffold planks and disappears, behind it the freshly painted facade appears: warm ochre plaster with crisp white stucco window surrounds and white cornices, the steel scaffold tubes and planks stay in place in front of the facade.
Second 7-10: soft golden morning sun hits the fully revealed fresh ochre facade, the white stucco details glow, clean sharp edges between ochre and white, the scaffold stands empty and clean, gentle clouds move across the sky.
Counts: exactly one building, exactly five storeys, the same number of windows in every frame, the scaffold structure keeps exactly the same shape from start to end.
Style: photorealistic cinematic architectural time-lapse, natural light, ultra sharp scaffold tubes, planks and plaster texture, stable geometry.
Avoid: camera movement, zoom, cuts, morphing building, changing number of windows or storeys, people, blurry ghost figures, cars driving through, text, signs, logos, watermark.
```

## Film 2 – Raum (First Frame f4-raum, 10 s, MiniMax H3 Unlimited)

```
One continuous shot, no cuts. Layout from left to right: the left corner of the room with a tall double window and a radiator, the back wall with a second tall double window, the right wall where a professional painter in his thirties in white painter's clothes stands in the right third holding a long-handled paint roller, about half of the right wall is already wet glossy deep teal, the rest primed white, the floor fully covered with grey protective fleece.
Camera: very slow steady dolly forward and slightly to the right toward the painter and the right wall, eye level, the painter stays in the right third of the frame the whole time, focus always on the roller and the wet paint.
Second 0-2: the painter presses the long roller onto the white wall next to the teal area and rolls one long vertical stroke from the stucco cornice down to the skirting board, the roller cover is soaked with paint, a wet glossy teal stripe appears.
Second 2-4: he rolls the roller back up in the same lane overlapping the edge, then a second vertical stroke one roller width further right, wet edge to wet edge, the teal surface stays smooth and even.
Second 4-6: he dips the roller in a paint bucket with a grid standing on the fleece at his feet, rolls off the excess on the grid, both hands stay on the long handle and stay visible.
Second 6-8: he rolls a third and fourth vertical stroke to the right, now almost the whole right wall is wet glossy deep teal with a satin sheen, a crisp straight line under the white stucco cornice.
Second 8-10: he finishes the last lane at the right corner, steps half a step back and holds the roller still, the camera arrives close to the fresh teal wall, soft daylight from the windows reflects on the wet paint.
Counts: exactly one painter, exactly one long-handled roller, exactly one paint bucket, exactly two tall double windows, the painter's hands never leave the roller handle.
Style: photorealistic cinematic interior, natural window daylight, ultra sharp roller texture and wet paint sheen, stable room geometry.
Avoid: cuts, jump in camera, second painter, roller changing shape, paint changing color, paint drips on the ceiling, morphing face or hands, text, logos, watermark.
```

## Film 3 – Karriere (First Frame f5-karriere, 10 s, Wan 3.0 Unlimited)

```
One continuous shot, no cuts. Layout from left to right: open sky and Berlin rooftops with the setting sun in the left half, a young female painter in her early twenties with a ponytail and a young male apprentice about nineteen years old, both in white painter's work clothes and white safety helmets, sitting side by side on the edge of a high steel scaffold platform with their legs dangling, scaffold tubes and planks in the right foreground.
Camera: very slow gentle push-in combined with a slight lateral drift to the left, steady, both painters stay sharp and in frame the whole time, focus always on the two painters.
Second 0-3: both laugh together naturally, the warm sun creates a soft lens flare, their legs swing slowly over the edge.
Second 3-6: the young woman points with her right hand at the skyline in the distance, the apprentice follows her gaze and nods, both smile.
Second 6-8: she lowers her hand onto the scaffold plank, the apprentice gives her a friendly fist bump, both keep their hands visible.
Second 8-10: both look out over the rooftops into the golden sunset, calm and proud, the light glows warmly on their faces and helmets.
Counts: exactly two people, exactly two helmets, they stay seated on the same spot of the scaffold platform from start to end.
Style: photorealistic cinematic golden hour, natural skin texture, soft lens flare, ultra sharp scaffold and clothing detail, stable faces.
Avoid: cuts, standing up, third person, morphing faces, changing clothes or helmets, extra fingers, falling, text, logos, watermark.
```
